# Owner instructions

The owner reaches the teams from inside the system, through one channel.

## The channel

An owner instruction is a file in `start-here/to-protean/` in the vault that carries either

- the file-name prefix `OWNER-`, or
- an explicit statement that the file relays the owner, verbatim.

Relayed instructions arrive through a Sparky agent: any Sparky may carry one. A file with neither marker is working material, not a directive, however urgent it reads.

## Handling

- **Highest priority.** An owner instruction is dispatched ahead of every queued task.
- **Verbatim.** The instruction text is passed on unsummarised, un-scoped and unparaphrased. A lane may add its own reading beneath the instruction, never in place of it.
- **Signed.** Like every other artifact, it carries the SOP name of whoever wrote it down (`procedure/LEADERSHIP-AND-SIGNOFF.md`).

## Limits

- **Relaying is not authority.** A Sparky that carries an owner instruction gains no authority beyond the relay role in `roster/ROSTER-AND-RELAY-NOTE.md`. The instruction carries the owner's authority; the carrier does not.
- **An owner instruction is not a packet.** It does not travel as SYM-2P and is not subject to the routing and act checks in `procedure/PHASE-0-MANUAL-RELAY.md`. The work it starts produces ordinary packets.
- **The intake boundary is unchanged.** Outside work enters through the orchestrator of the Protean team.
